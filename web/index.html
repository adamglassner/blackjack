How to Play Blackjack

Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Number cards count their number, J, Q and K count 10, and an Ace counts 1 or 11, whichever helps. A hand with an Ace counted as 11 is called soft.

  1. Bet. Click chips to build your bet in the circle, then press Deal. Your bet stays up for the next hand; press Clear to change it.
  2. The deal. You get two cards face up. The dealer gets one up and one face down. If the dealer shows an Ace or a ten, they check for Blackjack first.
  3. Hit takes another card. Stand keeps your total. Go over 21 and you bust, losing the bet.
  4. Double Down on your first two cards: double your bet and take exactly one more card.
  5. Split two cards of the same value into two hands, each with its own bet equal to the first. You can split up to four hands. Split Aces get one card each, and 21 after a split counts as 21, not Blackjack.
  6. The dealer turns over the hidden card, draws to 16 and stands on all 17s.
  7. Payouts. A win pays 1 to 1. A Blackjack (Ace and a ten-card on your first two cards) pays 3 to 2. A tie is a push and your bet comes back.

You start with 1,000 play chips and your balance is saved between sessions. If you run out, the house restocks you for free. These chips have no cash value.
