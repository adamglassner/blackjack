BLACKJACK - The Gold Room  (v@VERSION@)
An Ironite game. (c) 2026 Ironite.
==================================================

Open Blackjack from the Start menu (Ironite folder), your desktop, or the
Ironite Launcher. The game opens in its own window using Microsoft Edge
(built into Windows 10 and 11).

PLAY MONEY ONLY. Chips have no cash value. No real money, no purchases,
no accounts. You start with 1,000 chips and the house restocks you for
free if you run out.

HOW TO PLAY
- Click chips to bet, then Deal. Get closer to 21 than the dealer
  without going over. Aces count 1 or 11, face cards count 10.
- Hit (H), Stand (S), Double Down (D) on your first two cards, or Split
  (P) a pair into two hands, up to four.
- The dealer stands on all 17s. Blackjack pays 3 to 2, a regular win pays
  1 to 1, and a tie is a push.
- Choose Single Deck, Double Deck or a 6-Deck Shoe from the toolbar.
- M = music on/off, F11 = full screen.

Your chip balance, stats and settings are saved in
%APPDATA%\Ironite\Blackjack, outside the install folder, so updating or
reinstalling keeps them.

All card art, music and sounds are original and made by the game.
